1. About Room Stay

Room Stay is an online platform that connects landlords with prospective tenants in Witbank, Emalahleni and surrounding areas of Mpumalanga, South Africa. Room Stay is not a landlord or estate agent — we facilitate listings, introductions and (optionally) rent collection.

2. Landlord accounts and plans

Landlords must provide accurate personal information and a valid email. The Basic plan allows up to two (2) active listings for a fee of R50 per month. Additional listings or additional features may be offered at a different rate shown in your account. Room Stay reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that breach these terms, list false information or act fraudulently.

3. Property management (optional)

Where a landlord opts into Property Management, Room Stay collects rent on the landlord's behalf and pays the landlord their agreed net amount monthly. Room Stay adds its management fee (currently 15%, shown in the landlord's dashboard) on top of the landlord's amount — this fee is paid by the tenant, not deducted from the landlord. Properties under active Property Management are exempt from the R50 monthly plan fee while they remain managed. Either party may end Property Management at any time with one calendar month's written notice.

4. Listings and content

Landlords are solely responsible for the accuracy of their listings, including price, address, photos and amenities. By uploading photos you confirm you have the right to publish them. Room Stay reviews all listings before publication and may reject or remove any listing at its discretion. Public listings show a single all-in price that may include Room Stay's commission or management fee on top of the landlord's own price.

5. Tenant enquiries

Tenants submit viewing requests voluntarily. Room Stay passes the enquiry to the assigned agent and does not guarantee a viewing. Room Stay does not vet tenants and does not act as a party to any lease between the landlord and the tenant.

6. Payments and refunds

Plan fees are billed monthly in advance and are non-refundable once the month has started. Property Management rents are collected from tenants and paid to landlords on the agreed schedule. If a tenant fails to pay, Room Stay will make reasonable efforts to collect, but does not guarantee payment and will not advance funds to the landlord.
