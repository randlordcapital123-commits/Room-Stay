How property management works

By default, you list a room, set your price, and Room Stay adds a small commission on top. You collect the rent yourself. Your R50/month plan fee keeps your listing active.

If you tick "Let Room Stay manage this property", we take over the collection: the tenant pays Room Stay, and we pay you your full rental amount monthly. In return, we charge you no R50 monthly fee for that property. We earn a 15% management fee on top of your rent, paid by the tenant.

You can mix and match — one property managed, one not. No lock-in contract. You can switch a listing to or from managed at any time by editing it.
